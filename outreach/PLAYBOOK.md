# Outbound playbook (v1, Nov 2026)

Owner: Outbound Systems Strategist. Executed by Cold Outreach SDR. Read `business/aurelium-motion.md` first.

## ICP (falsifiable)
Owner-operated local service business, 2–25 staff, Greater Philadelphia metro, in a priority vertical (dental/ortho/medical → med spa/chiro/PT → law → home services → salons/vets/auto), **currently hiring front desk / receptionist / scheduler / office coordinator** or publicly losing calls.
Disqualify: franchises with central call centers, 50+ staff, outside metro, outbound-sales use cases, anyone requiring guarantees not in the business file.

## Signals and sources
| Tier | Signal | Where to find it | Contact within |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Live receptionist / front desk / patient coordinator / scheduler posting | Indeed, ZipRecruiter, LinkedIn Jobs, Glassdoor, Craigslist Philadelphia › admin/office, business careers pages | 48 hours |
| 1 | Review complaining about unanswered phones / voicemail | Google Maps reviews, Yelp | 48 hours |
| 2 | New second location, practice acquisition, "we're hiring" social post | Instagram, Facebook, local news, PA Dept of State filings | 1 week |
| 3 | ICP fit, no signal | Directories | Quarterly nurture only |

Search strings (rotate verticals): `"front desk" receptionist dental Philadelphia site:indeed.com`, `"patient coordinator" "Bala Cynwyd" OR "Ardmore" OR "King of Prussia"`, `receptionist "med spa" "Montgomery County" PA`, `"office coordinator" HVAC Philadelphia`, `receptionist "law firm" Philadelphia PA` plus Craigslist `philadelphia.craigslist.org/search/ofc?query=receptionist`.

## Sequence (Tier 1, 3 touches / 9 days)
- **T1 (Day 1)** — signal + offer. Subject: `your front desk opening` / `the receptionist role on indeed` / `calls while you're with patients`. ≤90 words. CTA: "Worth 15 minutes to call it yourself and hear how it handles a new-patient call?"
- **T2 (Day 4)** — the gap: lunch, after 5pm, during interviews and training of the new hire. New angle, no repeat ask wording.
- **T3 (Day 9)** — honest close: "I'll leave it here. If the search drags on, a 15-minute demo is the fastest way to see if this covers the gap."
Footer on every touch: Juan Jackson · Aurelium Motion · Philadelphia · [mailing address] · "Reply 'no' and I won't email again."

## Benchmarks
Reply 12%+ · Positive reply 5%+ · Demos 4+ per 100 emails · Bounce <2% · Complaints 0.
